One image that sums up the world's news of Wednesday, 18 November 2026:
38 stories across 6 continents, drawn as a dark-navy newspaper front page.

- 11 illustrated stories: Xi welcomes Trump at Shenzhen's airport, Bahrain
  and Kuwait intercept Iran's drones, Voyager 1 one light-day from Earth,
  Russia fires 141 drones at Ukraine, Fed minutes point to more hikes,
  Cyclone Akvan veers toward Odisha, RSF drones kill 37 in El Obeid, the EU
  proposes a €45bn Ukraine loan, UK inflation hits 3.6%, Israeli tanks roll
  into central Gaza, and Jeffries wins the Democrats' nod for House Speaker.
- 27 more briefs (12–38), plus a dotted world map with all 38 stories
  numbered at the places they happened, a stats strip, and a DATE CHECK box.
- Every story here happened or was first reported on Wednesday,
  18 November 2026. Sources: AP, Reuters, AFP, CNN, CNBC, NPR, Fed, ONS,
  IMD, NASA, Amnesty.

## Entry

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the bundled
`entry.png` to the output directory. It runs twice and writes identical
bytes both times. `entry.png` is a 1024×1024 PNG.
